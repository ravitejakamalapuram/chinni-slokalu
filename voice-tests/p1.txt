# AUDIO PROFILE: Janaki, a soft-spoken young Telugu teacher
## THE SCENE: A calm morning class, teaching a sloka to small children.
### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
Style: soft, warm, gentle, smiling. Pace: slow and clear. Chant the Sanskrit words slowly with a short pause between them, then a long pause before the Telugu meaning.
