Sloka reels: covers and videos

17 finished reels. Each card shows the cover. The buttons download the full video and the full-size cover from your Drive folder "Sloka reels (latest)".

  1. Download the video and the cover to your phone.
  2. In Instagram, add the video as a reel, then tap Edit cover and choose Add from camera roll.
  3. Pick the cover image and post.
